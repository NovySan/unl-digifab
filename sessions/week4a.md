## Shopbot and VCarve Workflow

We already covered ShopBot and table saw **safety** in [Week 3b](week3b.html#shopbot-and-table-saw) — the same rules apply today: this machine can hurt you, ask for help no matter what, there are no stupid questions, and borrow endmills from NovySan. Now we go deeper into the full large-format CNC workflow.

- From a Rhino model to a cut part:
  - Prepare your geometry in Rhino (curves and surfaces at the right scale/units).
  - Going from Rhino to VCarve Pro: [tutorial video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hb8g6G6hE4k)
  - [VCarve Pro free trial](https://www.vectric.com/free-trial/vcarve-pro)
- In VCarve:
  - Set your material size, thickness, and XY/Z origin.
  - Lay out your parts on the sheet; add tabs so parts don't shift when cut free.
  - Choose toolpaths: profile (cut out), pocket, and v-carve.
  - Select endmills and set feeds, speeds, pass depth, and roughing vs. finishing passes.
  - Preview the toolpaths, then save/export the toolpath file for the ShopBot.
- At the machine:
  - Secure your stock, zero the tool, and run the job with a spotter.
